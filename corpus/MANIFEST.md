## Traps

- **distractors** (D05, D06) — Identifiers formatted like money (client code, estimate no.) plus a prior-balance figure that is not the contract total.
- **prose** (D07, D08) — No table anywhere; every fact is inside a sentence.
- **eu_numbers** (D13, D14) — EUR with 1.234,56 separators — a decimal misparse changes the total by 100x.
- **sweep** (D15, D16) — Severity sweep control: same content as its sibling at another degradation level.
- **illegible** (D17) — Fax at worst severity: expected to be unreadable rather than misread.
- **cropped_total** (D18) — Photographed with the bottom of the page out of frame, so the total is genuinely absent. Correct behaviour is to report it missing.
- **inconsistent_total** (D19) — Line items do not sum to the printed contract total; a prose note mentions an adjustment. A human must decide which figure to book.
- **rotated_180** (D20) — Scanned upside down. Modern readers often cope; if it passes, that is a result worth reporting, not a failure of the test.
- **out_of_scope** (D21) — An office-relocation letter from a real sender that mentions a large dollar figure but is not an order at all.
